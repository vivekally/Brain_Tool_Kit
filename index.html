02 / Problem

Concussion Rehab Is Analog and Fragmented

The Gap

Concussion patients develop persistent oculomotor and vestibular symptoms requiring rehab beyond rest. Standard care uses low-tech methods (pen-and-paper, video demos) with no standardized way to assign, track, or adjust homework between visits. The founder frames this as "fragmented neuroperformance": intake tools lack training, vision therapy is disconnected from rehab, vestibular rehab has no app-based training, and consumer cognitive apps aren't clinically validated.

Key Assumptions

Grey Zone

Under both Health Canada and FDA, intended use and claims language determine classification. The more specific the clinical claims ("detect abnormal tilt"), the closer to SaMD. Current language sits in a grey area. I

12 / Summary

Assessment

Well Established

Broadest exercise set of any identified competitor (oculomotor + vestibular + cognitive + assessment). Genuine clinical/research credentials (PhD, systematic review, RCT, ACRM). 10+ years concussion-specific experience. Actively maintained through 2026.

Uncertain

Traction figures (3K+ units, 30+ countries) are founder-stated and unverifiable. Revenue composition unknown. Gap between 500+ Play downloads and 3K total plausible but unexplained. Primary user base (functional neurology niche vs. broader rehab) unclear.

Three Biggest Unknowns

1. Revenue & Unit Economics
Actual ARR, ARPU, and revenue split across app downloads, Pro subscriptions, and courses. Sustainable at current scale?
2. RCT Publication Timeline
ORCA trial is the single largest potential unlock for credibility and channel expansion into mainstream rehab medicine.
3. Team & Technical Capacity
Contract devs credited by initials, no co-founder or FTE engineering. Plan for clinician portal, sports features, sustained development?
